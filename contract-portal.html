How “Applied” works: Tapping Mark as applied moves the role out of the All Jobs list and into the Applied tab at the top, and saves the mark to the shared cloud database, so it syncs in real time to every phone or browser that opens this page — and it is still there after you close and reopen the page. A copy is also kept on this device as a fallback if the cloud connection is unavailable. Marking does not submit an application. Always apply on the actual LinkedIn / Dice posting via “View job.” In the Applied tab, tap ✓ Applied again to un-mark a role — it will move back to the All Jobs list on every device.

How stars work: Tapping the ☆ Star button marks a role as a priority — it gets a filled gold ★ and a “Priority” badge, and the “★ Starred only” filter shows just those roles. Stars sync across devices the same way Applied marks do, with a copy kept on this device as a fallback. Tap the star again to remove it everywhere.
